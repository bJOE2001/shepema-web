# 🐑 Shepema Android App — User Installation Guide

Welcome to the **Shepema** installation guide! Shepema is a beautiful, offline devotional buddy and Scripture reader designed to enrich your quiet time with God through the **R.R.M.A.** method.

---

## 📋 System Requirements

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| **Operating System** | Android 8.0 (Oreo) or higher (Android 8.0 - 15+) |
| **Storage Space** | ~110 MB free space (Supports downloadable offline Bible versions) |
| **Internet Connection** | Only needed once to download the APK. 100% offline afterwards! |
| **Permissions** | None required (No camera, microphone, contacts, or location access needed) |

---

## 🚀 Quick Step-by-Step Installation

### Step 1: Download the APK File
1. Visit the [Shepema Releases Page](https://github.com/bJOE2001/shepema-web/releases) or tap the download button on the website.
2. Click **[Shepema-v1.0.0.apk](https://github.com/bJOE2001/shepema-web/releases/download/v1.0.0/Shepema-v1.0.0.apk)** to start downloading.
3. If Google Chrome / your browser displays a notice saying:
   > *"File might be harmful. Do you want to download Shepema-v1.0.0.apk anyway?"*
   
   👉 Tap **"Download anyway"**. *(This is a generic security notice Android shows for any APK downloaded outside the Google Play Store).*

---

### Step 2: Open the Downloaded APK
1. When download completes, swipe down your notification tray and tap **Shepema-v1.0.0.apk**.
2. *Alternative:* Open your phone's **Files** or **My Files** app, navigate to your **Downloads** folder, and tap the APK file.

---

### Step 3: Allow Installation from Unknown Sources (If Prompted)
If this is your first time installing an APK via your browser or file manager:
1. A prompt will appear stating: *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source."*
2. Tap **Settings** on the prompt.
3. Turn **ON** the toggle for **"Allow from this source"**.
4. Tap the **Back (←)** button on your screen.

---

### Step 4: Install and Launch
1. Tap **Install** at the bottom right.
2. Wait a few seconds for the package installer to complete.
3. Tap **Open** to launch Shepema and begin your quiet time journey!

## 🛡️ Troubleshooting & FAQs

### Q1: Google Play Protect says "Unrecognized app" or "Blocked by Play Protect"?
**Answer:**
Google Play Protect scans applications. Because Shepema is distributed directly on GitHub and not on the Play Store yet, Play Protect may show an unverified developer warning.
1. Tap **"More details"** (or the small arrow).
2. Tap **"Install anyway"**.
3. Shepema contains zero ads, zero trackers, and does not collect personal data.

### Q2: Why is the APK file size ~108 MB?
**Answer:**
Shepema is built to work **100% offline**. It includes local databases and fast search indexing directly inside the app, allowing you to download and manage multiple Bible versions for complete offline reading and journaling anywhere.

### Q3: How do I update to future versions?
**Answer:**
When a new update is released, simply download and install the new APK over your existing Shepema app without uninstalling. Your journals, streaks, and bookmarks will remain completely safe!
